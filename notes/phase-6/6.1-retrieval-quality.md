## 6. Important Lesson

Retrieval finds the most similar information.

Retrieval does NOT automatically mean:

"this context contains enough information to answer the question."

Therefore:

Similarity ≠ sufficient evidence.

## 7. Similarity Threshold

We should NOT arbitrarily choose:

distance < 0.15

as our threshold.

Why?

Because distance values depend on:

- embedding model
- query
- chunk content
- domain
- chunking strategy
- knowledge-base distribution

We need more data before choosing a useful threshold.

## 8. Precision and Recall

Precision:

## Relevant retrieved chunks

1.  Precision (Are the results useful?)

- High Precision: Almost everything retrieved is useful and directly related.
- Low Precision: Lots of irrelevant "junk" is pulled up alongside useful info.

2.  Recall (Did it find everything?)

- High Recall: All truly relevant information in the database is successfully found.
- Low Recall: Crucial documents or important details are missed.
